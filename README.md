# Dulcețime - torturi de casă, pentru tine.

Landing page for my mom's business, where she takes orders for custom cakes from her customers.

## About 

I decided to create this landing page for my mom, Mariana, as her cakes are the tastiest and lightest ones I have ever tasted. She has been making cakes for years, using mainly natural ingredients and paying close attention to every detail. As baking is her passion, she puts her heart into everything she makes.

After talking to her, I convinced her that this page will help boost her business's visibility and support her dream of opening a pâtisserie, one day, that offers handmade goods made with fresh, local, and seasonal ingredients.

## Others

### CTA

![cta-mail-order-cake-request](https://files.catbox.moe/9g5jvp.png)

The *Call-To-Action* button is used to encourage the users to take action, in this case, to request a custom cake order, which once clicked it takes their form data and opens a `mailto` link to my mom's email, where they can send their order request.

### Meta-tags

![meta-tags](https://files.catbox.moe/wm65yg.png)

I have added meta-tags, such as the title, description, Open Graph tags, and Twitter/Facebook tags, together with the thumbnail image for the page, in order to improve the visibility of the page on search engines.

### Mascot

![mascot](https://files.catbox.moe/zwdoyt.gif)

I have created a mascot for the page, which is a cute cake with a smiling face, and I have added it to the page as an animated GIF, in order to make the page more visually appealing and to create a friendly atmosphere for the users. Hovering over it, it reveals the CTA button, which takes users to the *Build your own* section, where they can fill in their custom cake order.

## Technologies

- *HTML* - for structuring the content of the page
- *CSS* - for styling the page
- *Tailwind* - as a CSS framework, to speed up the styling process, and increase my HTML's [*readability*](https://files.catbox.moe/mx9qof.jpeg)
- *JS* - for interactivity with the navbar on mobile devices
- *Git* - for version control
- *GitHub* - for hosting the repository & for deploying the page

## Deployment

The website is currently deployed on *GitHub Pages*, using *GitHub Actions*.
- It is accessible at https://dulceti.me.

The domain has been purchased on *Namecheap*, and it is supposed to symbolize the word ***dulcețime***, which means ***sweetness*** in Romanian.
