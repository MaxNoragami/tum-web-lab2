# Dulcețime - torturi de casă, pentru tine.

Landing page for my mom's business, where she takes orders for custom cakes from her customers.

## About 

I decided to create this landing page for my mom, Mariana, as her cakes are the tastiest and lightest ones I have ever tasted. She has been making cakes for years, using mainly natural ingredients and paying close attention to every detail. As baking is her passion, she puts her heart into everything she makes.

After talking to her, I convinced her that this page will help boost her business's visibility and support her dream of opening a pâtisserie, one day, that offers handmade goods made with fresh, local, and seasonal ingredients. 

## Sections

The page is divided into 6 sections:

### Hero

![hero-section](https://files.catbox.moe/6qqbkr.png)

This section contains the main title of the page, a short description, an image of a cake, and a CTA button, which takes users to the <a href="#build-your-own">*Build your own* section</a>, where they can fill in their custom cake order.

### About me

![about-me-section](https://files.catbox.moe/c0433c.png)

This section contains a short description of my mom, the key person in this whole process of custom cake making.


### Gallery

![gallery-section](https://files.catbox.moe/2m7bw0.png)

This section contains a carousel of images of the cakes that my mom has made, both for custom orders and for our own family.

### How it works

![how-it-works-section](https://files.catbox.moe/qp3e3g.png)

In this section I have added a short description of the process of ordering a custom cake.

### <span id="build-your-own">Build your own</span>

![byoc-section](https://files.catbox.moe/nrxyia.png)

The *Build your own* section contains a form, where users can fill in their custom cake order, such as the shape, the fillings, etc.

![byoc-section-2](https://files.catbox.moe/44rolk.png)

At the end of the section, besides the form fields that request personal data, there is an *Order* CTA button, which once clicked it takes their form data and opens a `mailto` link to my mom's email, where they can send their order request.


### Contacts

![contacts-section](https://files.catbox.moe/y2wcvg.png)

The *Contacts* section contains the email address, which users can use in order to contact my mom for further information.

## Others

### CTA

![cta-mail-order-cake-request](https://files.catbox.moe/9g5jvp.png)

The *Call-To-Action* button is used to encourage the users to take action, in this case, to request a custom cake order, which once clicked it takes their form data and opens a `mailto` link to my mom's email, where they can send their order request.

### Meta-tags

![meta-tags](https://files.catbox.moe/wm65yg.png)

I have added meta-tags, such as the title, description, Open Graph tags, and Twitter/Facebook tags, together with the thumbnail image for the page, in order to improve the visibility of the page on search engines.

## Technologies

- *HTML* - for structuring the content of the page
- *CSS* - for styling the page
- *JS* - for interactivity with the navbar on mobile devices
- *Git* - for version control
- *GitHub* - for hosting the repository & for deploying the page

The website is currently deployed on *GitHub Pages*, using *GitHub Actions*.
